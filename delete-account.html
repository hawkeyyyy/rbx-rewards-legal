Delete your RBX Rewards account

RBX Rewards

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open RBX Rewards.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you have a gift card request in progress, cancel it or wait for it to finish first.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email rewardsrobux37@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my RBX Rewards account". Send it from the email address linked to your account, or include your display name and the Roblox username you used for rewards so we can find it. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, profile, display name, game progress and stats, Coins and Gems balances, pets, inventory, quests, achievements and notifications.

What is kept

Records of reward requests and the related transaction ledger are kept for up to 3 years for fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations, then deleted. Unredeemed Coins and Gems are lost and cannot be restored.
