Privacy Policy

RBX Rewards · Last updated 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information the RBX Rewards app ("RBX Rewards", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Contact: supportrbxrewards@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. A random account ID created when you first open the app. If you choose to link an account, the email address and name from Google Sign-In or the email address you register with.
  • Profile. Your display name, chosen pets and cosmetics, and notification preference. Your display name is shown to other players on leaderboards and in the public rewards feed.
  • Gameplay and balances. Game sessions and scores, play time, quests, tasks, streaks, achievements, and your Coins and Gems balances and transaction history.
  • Reward requests. The Roblox username you enter when you request a gift card, the request history, and the gift card assigned to you. We never ask for your Roblox password, 2-step verification codes or cookies. Never enter them in the app.
  • Device and anti-fraud data. A salted hash of a device identifier, app version, and risk signals (for example, several accounts on one device) used to prevent abuse of rewards. We use Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity) to confirm requests come from the genuine app.
  • Advertising data. Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, according to your consent choices.
  • Analytics and crash data. If you allow it, Firebase Analytics collects usage events. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and device information to help us fix bugs.
  • Notifications. If you enable notifications, a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can tell you when a reward is ready.

How we use information

  • To run the app: keep your progress, verify game sessions, credit Coins and Gems, and process gift card requests.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating and multi-account farming, and to enforce our Terms.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and to verify rewarded ads with Google.
  • To understand how the app is used, improve it and fix problems.
  • To send you notifications you have turned on, and to reply when you contact us.

Advertising and consent

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where required (for example, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads or analytics are used. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Watching ads never earns Coins.

Service providers

We use Google services to operate the app: Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting, Remote Config, App Check, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) and Google AdMob. They process data on our behalf under Google's terms; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data is stored in the United States. We do not sell your personal information.

Gift cards

Gift card codes are stored encrypted and are only shown to the account they were issued to. RBX Rewards is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation. Roblox gift cards are redeemed on Roblox's own website under Roblox's terms.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, progress, balances, inventory and notifications are deleted. We keep reward request records and the related transaction ledger for up to 3 years for fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. Anonymous accounts with no activity may be deleted after a period of inactivity, and unused Coins expire as described in the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account, or request deletion on our account deletion page.
  • Turn analytics, personalised ads and notifications on or off in the app and in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, or object to its use. Email us to make a request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

RBX Rewards is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Balances and rewards can only be changed by our servers, and gift card codes are encrypted at rest. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
